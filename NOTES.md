# NOTES — cars-7seat site

Last updated: 2026-10-20 (Asia/Jerusalem).

## Structure
- `index.html` landing · `specs.html` sortable/filterable table (cards on mobile) · `photos.html` to-scale side photos (lineup / overlay / schematic front / bars) · `viewer3d.html` Three.js viewer (click-to-load GLBs, progress bar, runtime rescale) · `credits.html`.
- Pages are generated by `src/build.py` from `src/*.body.html` (shared header/footer). Data: `assets/data.js` (specs, prices, NCAP, reviews), `assets/media.js` (photo sources, 3D credits + baked dims).
- three.js 0.160 vendored in `vendor/three` (no CDN). GLBs in `models/` (meshopt + webp, 0.7–2.6 MB each, ~8.3 MB total).

## Data decisions (verified 20.10.2026)
- MG S9 combined power 299 PS / 220 kW (sheet had ~231 = e-motor only). Source: MG UK brochure June 2026; prices mg-israel.co.il.
- Kia Carnival height 1785 (kia-israel brochure); sheet/scale_report had 1775. 3D model rescaled at runtime (baked dims kept in media.js).
- Tiggo 8 Pro petrol: importer lists "147/5,000" under hp — it's kW (≈200 PS). Displayed with explanation.
- Tiggo 8 Pro PHEV: no official combined output; show 143+204.
- Tiggo 9 Pro: boot 143/819/2065, EV 75 (FWD) / 148 (AWD) per importer brochure. AWD combined output not published (≈428 like Jaecoo 8, labelled as such).
- Euro NCAP Tiggo 8: 1223ra reassessment 5★ (82/85/80/78), 15.10.2025; based on EU Tiggo 7/8.
- Santa Fe NCAP 2024: 4★ standard / 5★ with safety pack. Carnival, Tiggo 9, Jaecoo 8: no Euro NCAP rating.
- MIFA 7 has more than one importer; official Maxus price list 3.6.2026 used, others noted.
- Santa Fe Luxury and Ioniq 9 fee-inclusive prices not published → shown as list price only.

## Images
- All side photos are official manufacturer/importer images (MG UK brochure, kia-israel, hyundaimotors.co.il, umimaxus, cheryisrael) or manufacturer press images via ZigWheels (Jaecoo 8, Tiggo 9). Watermarked images (CARICOS, BitAuto) from the earlier prototype were dropped.
- Background removed with rembg (isnet-general-use); all face left (front at x=0). Height-vs-spec discrepancy 1.9–4.6 %.
- No free-licence true frontal photos were found for all models → "front" view is schematic (W×H outlines from spec). Billboards for MG S9 / MIFA 7 in 3D use side photo + front outline.

## Privacy
- Only public specs/list prices/NCAP/reviews. Sheet section B (personal history), column S and personal remarks in O/Q were excluded; the sheet URL is not published.
- Scan: keyword grep (names, leasing, phone/email patterns, tax terms) + OCR (tesseract heb+eng) of all published images and GLB textures — clean. See `/workspace/cars-7seat-audit/AUDIT.md`.
